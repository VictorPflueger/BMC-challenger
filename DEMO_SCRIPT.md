# Demo script: five minutes

Tidewell Lines, the default scenario: a fictional container line from Bremen. Every figure below is the
engine's, as in the guide's talk track.

## Before you start

- Open the live site in a fresh tab, full screen, at 1920 × 1080 or 1280 × 720. *How it works* opens by
  itself; `?guide=present` opens it on the talk track.
- Close the guide with **Start**, then press **Start the agents**. The run plays and stops where you
  act. Space pauses; the arrow keys or a clicker move a step.
- No key needed; the simulation works offline.

## The five minutes

| Time | Step | Say | Do and watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 min | Point at the key figures | "Tidewell Lines has €32.9m a year at stake and 6 of 9 blocks under pressure. A live run like this would cost ≈ €0.05." | Point at the three tiles; four empty blocks ask their question. |
| 1 min | Start and Complete: the AI's additions | "The team filled five blocks. The agents added 10 items in lilac, and each says what it rests on." | Click **Start the agents**. Open a lilac item: its basis and confidence. Its × takes it out, **Keep** brings it back. |
| 1 min | Challenge: the first issue | "Emission allowances raise the cost of every European call, and the source is the Commission's own page." | Press **Challenge this canvas**: six issues, 6 of 9 blocks under pressure. Show the first issue's source; at Assess it risks €12.6m a year. |
| 1 min | Adapt: choose and apply | "Pass the cost on, plan both routes, sell an emissions statement: €7.8m is left a year, and 2 of 9 blocks stay under pressure." | Switch one issue to **Accept**: the bar's figure rises. Switch back, then **Apply to the canvas**: the tiles strike €32.9m and 6 of 9 through. |
| 1 min | Summary: the report, then your company | "Download the report: every page carries its footer, and every canvas the Strategyzer credit. Agents wrote the words, code computed every figure. Run it live does the same for your company, on your own Claude key." | **Download the report**, then **Save as PDF** with Save as PDF as the printer. Point at **Run it live** and its AI notice. |

## If something goes wrong

- After a wrong click, **Keep** brings a removed item back; after applying, **Change your choices** reopens
  the decision.
- Lost on the way? `?scenario=s1&step=4` opens Adapt, `step=5` the Summary with the recommended responses
  applied.
- No page numbers? Print from Chrome or Edge; Firefox and Safari leave them out.
- On a small projector the page fits 1280 × 720: lower the resolution, don't zoom.
- Only "Starting…" on screen? The page was opened from a file: open the live site.

## Questions that will come

**Is this real?** Tidewell Lines and its own figures are invented. The rules and markets it meets are
real and dated: from 2026 ships surrender emission allowances for all the emissions EU rules count,
FuelEU Maritime tightens the fuel mix, Asia–Europe loops return to Suez. DATA_SOURCES.md lists each source.

**Where do the figures come from?** From code. The agents rate each issue and estimate a euro range; the
engine works out every key figure, and a fact audit refuses numbers in the agents' words.

**What does "under pressure" mean?** An issue rated medium or worse touches the block; with only low
issues it is on watch, marked with an eye.

**Is the canvas Strategyzer's?** Yes, released under CC BY-SA 3.0, with "Strategyzer.com" under every
canvas.

**What does a run cost?** About €0.05 on Balanced with research, under €0.01 without, both estimated;
**Run it live** uses your own Claude key, up to a cap you set.
